I am a PhD candidate at the Chair for Energy, Comfort and Health in Buildings at the Technical University of Berlin. I work at the Hermann-Rietschel-Institut with Prof. Martin Kriegel, studying how computational fluid dynamics and artificial intelligence can help us understand indoor air quality.

My interests include neural operators, deep learning, and ventilation analysis. Before my PhD, I completed two master's degrees at the Technical University of Munich and a bachelor's degree at Beijing Institute of Technology. My previous work includes CFD studies of air quality in vehicle cabins at Audi AG and Bayesian optimization for turbulence modeling.
