I am a research associate and PhD candidate at the Chair for Energy, Comfort and Health in Buildings (Hermann-Rietschel-Institut, HRI) at the Technical University of Berlin, supervised by Prof. Martin Kriegel. My research focuses on computational fluid dynamics and artificial intelligence for indoor air quality.

My interests include neural operators, deep learning, and ventilation analysis. I hold two master's degrees from the Technical University of Munich, in Medical Engineering and Assistance Systems and in Mechatronics and Robotics, and a bachelor's degree from Beijing Institute of Technology.
